# Music handoff sheet

Generated by `python tools/content/music_handoff.py` from the route map, `content/story/music.json` and the packs; regenerate after changing any of them. What every scene of every world plays today, what is still a placeholder, and what to export next. How to import: `docs/MUSIC_IMPORT.md`.

## What each scene plays now

A **world** is a small graph of **scenes**; each scene is one 64-beat phase at a place in the world and names an arrangement and a slot of it (`content/story/music.json`, one entry per scene; `content/music/arrangements/`). The arrangement's 16-bar window `slot` is what the scene plays: one 16-bar section or two 8-bar ones, each a pack of the arrangement's family with a mix recipe. The entry scene opens on the world's base pack, the exit scene ends on a drop and an ending. The pack named in the table is the first section's (the chart of block 0, what the route lock compares); the sections column lists the window, and `alternates` are the other arrangements the per-run seed may pick instead (same family, same slots). A scene plays tier min(depth, 4) of its chart pack (depth is per world). Sibling scenes offered in one route window that chart different packs fall under the route lock (the chart is fixed 3.5 beats before the phase ends and the other routes grey out). `bass` says how the bass cues were timed at import: *audio checked* (the audio agrees with the bass MIDI) or *midi fallback* (charted from the MIDI timing).

## Format reminder

- One **set** per level: `Beat Shaper - <project>-Drums`, `-Bass` and `-Synth`, each as **.wav and .mid**. Variants keep the ` (1)`, ` (2)` suffix on all three files.
- **Needed:** Drums.wav/.mid and Bass.wav/.mid. A missing Synth is imported as a silent backing; the Synth MIDI is never read.
- **Length:** 16 quarter notes; rendered at 87 BPM (32-beat phrase) or 174 BPM (16-beat phrase); the importer measures which. A MIDI that ends a quarter short or long is fine.
- **Drum MIDI:** kick 36, snare 38/40; when none of those are present the most common low and mid notes (hats skipped) are used and the choice is reported. `--kick`/`--snare` override.
- **Bass MIDI:** every note is a hold on a pad chosen by pitch; if the audio and the MIDI disagree on timing the MIDI wins, with a warning (`--trust-midi` forces it).
- Check a folder before importing: `python tools/content/beatshaper.py survey NewMusic`.
