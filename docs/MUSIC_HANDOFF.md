# Music handoff sheet

Generated by `python tools/content/music_handoff.py` from the route map and the packs; regenerate after changing either. What every level plays today, and exactly what to export to replace it.

## What the game needs from a set

- One **set** per level: `Beat Shaper - <project>-Drums`, `-Bass` and `-Synth`, each as **.wav and .mid** (six files), the format you already export. Variants of the same project keep the ` (1)`, ` (2)` suffix on all three of their files.
- **Length:** 16 quarter notes of music. The importer works out whether the render is at 87 BPM (a quarter is two game beats, the Lv1 Warehouse profile) or at 174 BPM (a quarter is one game beat, the Lv2 Alien Spaceship profile) by measuring the drum recording against the MIDI; it refuses any other tempo and says so. 87 BPM gives a 32-game-beat phrase (11.03 s), 174 BPM a 16-game-beat phrase (5.52 s); both tile the 64-beat phase.
- **Release tails** are welcome (up to one phrase): they are folded onto the start of the loop. Stems may have different lengths.
- **Drum MIDI:** kick = note 36, snare = note 40 (38 also works). Hats and anything else stay in the audio but are not cues. A set that uses other notes can be imported with `--kick`/`--snare`.
- **Bass MIDI:** every note becomes a hold on a pad chosen by pitch (lowest note on pad 1). A long line is thinned for the early levels.
- **Synth** is backing: it comes in quietly with the bass.
- **Where to put them:** anywhere (a folder such as `NewMusic/`, or a ZIP). Nothing in the folder is modified.
- **Import and assign (one command per level):** `python tools/content/beatshaper.py apply NewMusic --node <node id> --set "<project>" --id <pack id>`. Add `--audio-only` for a sibling that is an alternate render of the same MIDI. Then `python tools/content/beatshaper.py validate`.

## How many sets to replace everything

- Chart sets: **4** (one per depth: Lv1, Lv2, Lv3, Lv4).
- Alternate renders (optional, for the sibling sound): **3**.
- That is 4 to 7 sets, 24 to 42 files.
