## Art

- 746 PNG frames copied from `godot_project/client/hybrid/art/openra/` (sha256 over all frames in name order: `8af7a60bc90c4c07e96bc58345c6d971857bad9adb3b2e58cda2e1e5e00e5666`).
- The frames were extracted from the owner's own Red Alert content packages (`conquer.mix` and friends) with OpenRA.Utility and the project's own `mix_read.py`/`shp_td.py`, rendered with the `temperat` palette. The source catalogue records that use was permitted for this project. The OpenRA engine is GPL-3.0-or-later; the original Red Alert artwork is not covered by that licence (Electronic Arts released Red Alert as freeware in 2008).
- Player-colour remap: temperat.pal indices 80..95 are baked in as the exact RGB values listed in `catalogue.json` (`remap_ramp_rgb`).
- Catalogue ids kept: openra.1tnk, openra.2tnk, openra.3tnk, openra.e1, openra.heli, openra.hpad, openra.powr, openra.fact, openra.weap, openra.barr, openra.barracks, openra.brik, openra.sbag, openra.gun, openra.agun, openra.sam, openra.ftur, openra.piff, openra.explosion, openra.120mm.
